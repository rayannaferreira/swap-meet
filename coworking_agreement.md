# Coworking Agreement

Talk through each section with your partner. Add notes on what you discussed and agreed upon in each section. At the bottom, type your names to sign off on your agreement.

## Accessibility Needs
We will communicate openly if either of us needs extra time, clarification, or support. If one of us has a question or problem, we will try to solve it together.

## Collaboration vs. Individual Work Expectations
We prefer to divide the project into two independent parts. One person will work through Wave 3, and the other person will work from Wave 3 through Wave 6.

Even though we are dividing the work, we will help each other if we have questions or problems.

All commits and pushes will be with both partners present. We will review the changes together before pushing them.

## Learning Style
We prefer to work independently on our assigned parts and then come together to review our progress.

We will meet during class breaks Tuesday 09/22 and Wednesday 09/23 to work together until the project is completed.

## Preferred Feedback Style
We are both open to feedback. At the end of each Wave, we will review the work together and discuss anything that needs to be changed before submitting or moving forward.

## One Team Communication Skill to Improve
We want to improve our communication by keeping each other updated about our progress and clearly explaining any changes we make to the project.

## Optional: Other agreements
We will use slack as our main communication tool.  We will keep a written record of our progress in our slack group or conversation, including updates about changes made to the project.  We will practice presenting and explaining our code together so that both partners understand the complete project and are prepared for the presentation.
